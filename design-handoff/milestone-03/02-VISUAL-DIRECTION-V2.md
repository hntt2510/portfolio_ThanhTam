# Scene 03 V2 — Core Expertise | Visual Direction & Atmospheric Design V2

## 1. Visual Philosophy: Beyond the Static Gallery

The core failure of Milestone 03 V1 was treating Scene 03 as a continuation of the same presentation format: another circular gallery with an eye-level camera and a static 42%-left text box.

**Scene 03 V2 transforms the experience into "Expertise in Motion"** — a dynamic architectural journey through multi-level elevations, distinct volumetric shapes, physical spatial metaphors, and varied editorial compositions.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Material Specifications

The Glass & Light Museum identity is preserved while introducing rich material contrast through basalt pathway inlays and frosted structural cubes:

```
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│   Warm Plaster  │ Light Stone     │  Honed Basalt   │ Brushed Silver  │  Frosted Glass  │
│     #F8F8F5     │     #F4F2EC     │     #52565C     │     #EEF3F8     │     #D7E8F6     │
│ (Sawtooth Walls)│ (Main Travertine)│ (Process Paths) │  (System Spine) │  (Modular Cubes)│
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

- **Warm-White Plaster (`#F8F8F5`):** Matte surface for perimeter walls and sawtooth ceiling baffles ($R = 0.82$, $M = 0.00$). Adsorbs and reflects daylight softly without specular glare.
- **Light Travertine Limestone (`#F4F2EC`):** Base floor slabs with subtle natural veining ($R = 0.18$, $Clearcoat = 0.45$).
- **Honed Basalt Stone (`#52565C`):** Contrasting dark stone strips ($10\text{cm} \to 100\text{cm}$ wide) embedded into the floor representing the As-Is $\to$ To-Be process lines ($R = 0.30$, $M = 0.05$).
- **Brushed Silver Aluminum (`#EEF3F8`):** Structural beams and mullions for the 3-tier System Spine and balustrade rails ($M = 0.82$, $R = 0.20$).
- **Translucent Frosted Glass (`#D7E8F6`):** Semi-opaque architectural cubes housed within the System Spine ($Transmission = 0.85$, $Roughness = 0.35$). Injects crisp ice-blue luminance into the core of the installation.

## 3. Sawtooth Roof & Natural Daylight Optics

Unlike the circular dome oculus of Scene 01, Scene 03 V2 employs an **industrial-modern sawtooth roof structure** inspired by contemporary civic museums (such as the Whitney Museum or Renzo Piano's Menil Collection):

1. **North-Facing Sawtooth Clerestories:**
   Four angled ceiling bays inclined at $35^\circ$, each crowned with vertical north-facing clear glass clerestories. This admits steady, shadowless $6500\text{K}$ natural skylight that washes smoothly down the tall 10.5m north wall.
2. **Directional Morning Sunlight:**
   Low-angle sunlight ($5200\text{K}$, $48^\circ$ altitude, $24^\circ$ azimuth) filters through high east facade apertures, casting geometric diagonal shadow stripes across the System Spine and floor pathways.
3. **Multi-Plane Parallax:**
   Because elements exist across varying heights ($Z = 0.0\text{m}$ floor, $Z = 2.4\text{m}$ bridge, $Z = 4.5\text{m}$ spine top, $Z = 10.5\text{m}$ roof), scrolling creates genuine 3D parallax impossible in a simple flat corridor.
